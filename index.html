COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you download.

Is UAScribe a flight-control or LAANC app?

No. UAScribe documents flights and creates quotes and reports. It does not control your aircraft or grant airspace authorization.

Can I print a completed report?

Yes. Save, share, or print the completed PDF through the iOS share sheet. Printing requires a compatible printer.

Can I use UAScribe on an iPad?

Yes. The app supports iPhone and iPad, including landscape layouts on iPad.

Will records automatically sync between my devices?

Saved records are local to the device where you create them. Do not rely on automatic iPhone-to-iPad synchronization. Save important report PDFs where you can access them later.

How do the three free reports work?

Try three completed mission reports before purchasing the permanent unlock. Canceling the share sheet does not use a trial report, and sharing an unchanged completed report again does not count it twice.
