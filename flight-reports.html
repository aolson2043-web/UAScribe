Time The Flight. Keep The Duration In The Report

Start and stop UAScribe’s built-in flight timer as you document the operation. The recorded duration carries into the mission report. With a compatible paired Apple Watch, you can send start and stop commands from your wrist while the iPhone keeps the flight record.

UAScribe also maintains running flight-time totals for each aircraft. Save an unfinished flight in History when you need to return and complete the record later.

Keep A Useful UAV Flight Log

A useful flight record brings together the date, mission, aircraft, pilot, location, and flight time. Use the built-in timer to record flight duration, complete the mission details, and return to saved records in History. Automatic DJI or Autel telemetry imports are not included.

Document The Mission While It’s Fresh

Record the remote pilot in command (RPIC), aircraft, crew, mission details, flight times, and communication plan. Use the dedicated UAV preflight checklist to keep the review with the operation.

Add Context From Your Location

Request coordinates, nearby airport information, surface-airspace information, and National Weather Service observations using the device’s current location or an address you enter. Online lookups depend on service availability.

UAScribe supports documentation. Airspace and weather information are informational and do not grant flight authorization or replace your preflight responsibilities.
