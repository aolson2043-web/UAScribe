Help With UAScribe

Find answers about reports, quotes, purchases, and your saved records.

Which devices can I use?

UAScribe supports iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later.

How does the trial work?

Create and share 3 mission reports for free. After the trial, a one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited mission reports. No subscription is required. See the App Store listing for the current purchase price.

Do I need an account?

No online account is required. Your saved records are stored locally on your device.

Does UAScribe work without an internet connection?

You can work with local records on your device. Live weather, airport, airspace, and geocoding lookups require a connection. Purchasing and restoring the Pro unlock also require access to Apple’s services.

Can I include a quote with a flight report?

Yes. Link a saved quote to the flight, then choose to include the linked quote in the completed PDF.

Does the app provide airspace authorization?

No. Lookup information supports your documentation and planning; it does not grant authorization to fly.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in UAScribe and use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Will records automatically sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Records are stored locally. Do not rely on automatic cross-device synchronization; save important documents as PDFs and save copies where you need them.

Can I control the flight timer with Apple Watch?

Yes. When UAScribe is installed on a compatible, paired Apple Watch, you can view the flight timer and send start or stop commands to your iPhone. Your iPhone remains the source of the saved flight record.

Does UAScribe keep a running flight-time total for each aircraft?

Yes. The latest version totals the time from flights you document for each aircraft. You can review accumulated flight time in that aircraft’s maintenance section alongside its service history. This is separate from automatic DJI or Autel telemetry importing, which is not included.

Does the built-in timer add flight duration to the report?

Yes. Start and stop the timer to record the flight duration, which carries into the mission report. A compatible paired Apple Watch can send start and stop commands to your iPhone.

Can I save an unfinished flight and complete it later?

Yes. Save the flight record and return to it from History when you are ready to finish documenting the mission.
