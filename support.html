UAV Flight Documentation

Need help using UAScribe? Support is available for flight documentation, quotes, aircraft records, PDF exports, location permissions, weather information, and purchase restoration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Location or weather is not loading
Confirm that Location Services are enabled for UAScribe in the iPhone or iPad Settings app. An internet connection is required to retrieve current weather information.

Restoring a previous purchase
Open UAScribe, go to Settings, and select Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account that originally purchased the upgrade.

Exporting a report
Complete the applicable flight information, generate the report, and use the Apple share sheet to save, print, or send the PDF.

Data and aircraft records
UAScribe stores mission, quote, aircraft, and maintenance records locally on the user's device.
