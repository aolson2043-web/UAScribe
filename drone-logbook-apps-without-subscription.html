Start With The Work You Need The Logbook To Do

A client-ready mission report and an analysis of aircraft telemetry solve different problems. Before comparing prices, decide whether you need manual flight documentation, automatic flight-data imports, a shared fleet system, or a combination. A tool can be useful without replacing every other aviation service you use.

Where UAScribe Fits

Choose UAScribe when you want to document the operation, time flights on iPhone or a compatible paired Apple Watch, keep automatic flight-time totals by aircraft, create service quotes, and share or print a branded PDF. Save flight drafts in History to complete later. The permanent Pro unlock covers unlimited mission reports. Saved records remain local to your device.

UAScribe does not automatically import DJI or Autel telemetry, control the aircraft, provide LAANC authorization, or provide a shared cloud fleet dashboard. If those are your main requirements, evaluate a service built around them.

Questions To Ask Before Choosing A Drone Flight-log App

  • Do I want to type a mission record or import aircraft telemetry?
  • Do I need a report for the customer, the agency, or my own history?
  • Is the quoted price per pilot, per aircraft, per month, or a permanent purchase?
  • Which features are included in the free tier and which require payment?
  • Where are my records saved, and what happens if I replace a device or stop paying?
  • Can I keep the records and supporting documents I need for my workflow?
