Where UAScribe fits.

Choose UAScribe when you want to enter the operation’s details, keep a practical flight history, create service quotes, and hand over a branded PDF. The permanent Pro unlock covers unlimited mission reports. Saved records remain on the device where you create them; online weather, address, and airspace lookups still use external services.

UAScribe does not automatically import DJI or Autel telemetry, control the aircraft, provide LAANC authorization, or provide a shared cloud fleet dashboard. If those are your main requirements, evaluate a service built around them.

Questions to ask before choosing a drone flight-log app.

  • Do I want to type a mission record or import aircraft telemetry?
  • Do I need a report for the customer, the agency, or my own history?
  • Is the quoted price per pilot, per aircraft, per month, or a permanent purchase?
  • Which features are included in the free tier and which require payment?
  • Where are my records saved, and what happens if I replace a device or stop paying?
  • Can I keep the records and supporting documents I need for my workflow?

Try a manual record first.

A printable preflight checklist or maintenance log template helps you identify which fields you actually use. Then try UAScribe’s three free reports and judge the completed PDF against your own work.
