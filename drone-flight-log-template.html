FREE EXCEL RESOURCE

Drone Flight Log Spreadsheet

A straightforward logbook for individual drone flights, with totals for minutes and decimal hours.

Get the Excel flight log

200 prepared rows. Dropdowns, filters, and a Guide sheet. No signup required.

What To Record

Use one row per flight. Enter the date, mission reference, mission type, aircraft ID, RPIC, general location, actual duration in minutes, light conditions, and notes. Use consistent aircraft identifiers so you can filter the records later.

What The Spreadsheet Calculates

The log counts entered durations, sums minutes, and converts the total to decimal hours. Select Day, Twilight, Night or Mixed yourself; the sheet does not determine sunrise, sunset, or regulatory operating conditions.

Keep A History You Can Use

Save a copy in your chosen storage location and follow your organization’s backup procedures. The workbook starts blank. Expand the table and summary ranges when you need more than 200 rows.

This is a standalone spreadsheet, not an automatic UAScribe import format.
