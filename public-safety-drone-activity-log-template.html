Public Safety Drone Activity Log Template

A clear place to document the mission, and a separate worksheet for activity your agency approves to share.

Designed for law enforcement, fire, search and rescue, and emergency-management drone operators.

Get the two-page PDF

Fillable PDF. US Letter. No signup required.

Page 1: An Internal Mission Record

Record the agency, mission reference, tasking, RPIC and crew roles, aircraft, flight activity, preflight context, factual observations, and follow-up needs. Use agency-approved identifiers and systems for sensitive information.

Page 2: An Activity-summary Worksheet

Prepare broad mission categories, general areas, flight counts, durations, and an approved activity summary. Keep this worksheet separate from the internal record and have the appropriate agency reviewer approve any release.

This template does not decide what is legally releasable. Avoid including personal information, sensitive locations, tactics, evidence identifiers, or restricted incident details in a public summary.

Make Each Record Useful After The Flight

A consistent record supports supervisor review, training discussions, equipment follow-up, and crew handoffs. Follow your organization’s retention, access, and release procedures.
