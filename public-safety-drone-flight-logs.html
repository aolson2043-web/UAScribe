WHY DOCUMENTATION MATTERS

The Flight Ends. The Questions May Come Later

A useful flight record preserves the purpose, people, conditions, and timing behind a mission. It gives the next reviewer more than a recollection of what happened.

SUPERVISOR REVIEW

Explain The Mission Clearly

Keep the mission purpose, location, crew roles, flight times, and factual notes together so a supervisor can review the operation in context.

AFTER-ACTION DISCUSSIONS

Learn From The Operation

A consistent record supports discussions about preparation, coordination, and observations. Distinguish what you observed from conclusions drawn afterward.

TRAINING HISTORY

Keep Training Visible

Identify exercises and training flights alongside operational missions. Build a history you can refer to when reviewing the work your operators have performed.

AIRCRAFT & CREW HANDOFFS

Carry The Follow-up Forward

Keep aircraft flight-time totals and maintenance entries available. Record equipment concerns and unresolved needs for the next operator to review.

A RECORD YOU CAN RETURN TO

Keep The Essentials Connected

  • Mission identifier, purpose, date, and location
  • Remote pilot in command, crew roles, and aircraft
  • Preflight review and relevant conditions
  • Flight durations and mission notes
  • Aircraft hours and maintenance history
  • A completed PDF to save, share, or print

Use factual notes and your agency’s approved naming conventions. A training log helps organize history; it does not establish operator proficiency or agency approval by itself.

YOUR RECORDS. YOUR SHARING DECISIONS.

Local Records, With Deliberate Sharing

No UAScribe account is required. Saved flight and maintenance records stay on the device where you create them, with no developer-operated cloud database receiving those records. You choose when to share a completed PDF.

How UAScribe fits an agency’s recordkeeping process

UAScribe prepares an individual operator’s records. It does not provide a shared agency database, evidence management, chain-of-custody tracking, automatic aircraft telemetry imports, or flight authorization.

Follow your agency’s procedures for approved devices, permitted information, access, backups, retention, and sharing. Keep sensitive evidence in approved systems. Requested online lookups, device backups, and chosen sharing destinations have their own data practices. Read the privacy policy.
