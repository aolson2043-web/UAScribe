A repeatable record for each job.

If you fly drones for client work, a clear record can help you explain what happened on a job and find those details later. UAScribe brings manually entered mission details, aircraft, RPIC, crew, flight times, and supporting context into a report you can save or share.

This page describes a documentation workflow, not a list of universal legal recordkeeping requirements. Choose the records appropriate to your operation, authorizations, and customer needs.

Before the flight: establish the context.

Start with the mission and aircraft, identify the remote pilot in command and crew, and record the location and communication plan. Link a saved customer quote when it is relevant. Use the optional preflight checklist to document your preparation.

During the job: capture useful details.

Enter flight times and mission details while they are fresh. When requested, location-based lookups can provide weather, nearby-airport, and surface-airspace information. Online lookups depend on service availability.

After the flight: keep and share the record.

Review the completed information, create a PDF with your organization name and logo, and optionally include the linked quote. Share or save the report through the iOS share sheet.

Documentation support. Pilot responsibility.

UAScribe does not issue a Remote Pilot Certificate, grant airspace authorization, control your aircraft, or determine whether a flight complies with Part 107. Its records and informational lookups support your workflow; the pilot remains responsible for flight decisions and applicable requirements.

For authoritative information, consult the FAA’s resources for certificated remote pilots and Remote Pilot Certificate guidance.

Does it import aircraft flight logs automatically?

No. UAScribe is built around the flight details you enter and the reports you create. It does not automatically sync DJI or Autel flight logs.

Yes. Try 3 reports for free, then choose whether to create unlimited reports with a one-time Pro unlock. No monthly subscription or UAScribe account is required.
