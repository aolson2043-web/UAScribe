What Belongs On A UAV Preflight Checklist?

Use these categories as a starting point, then add the checks required for your aircraft and mission.

01 / AIRCRAFT

Inspect The Aircraft And Equipment

  • Aircraft condition and visible damage
  • Propeller condition and attachment
  • Flight battery and available power
  • Controller and mobile-device charge
  • Aircraft system readiness
  • Remote ID status
02 / SITE & AIRSPACE

Review The Operating Area

  • Launch and recovery area
  • Weather and local conditions
  • Airspace and required authorization
  • Obstacles along the flight path
  • Nearby people and vehicles
  • Planned route and recovery options
03 / CREW

Confirm Operational Readiness

  • Remote pilot readiness
  • Crew roles and briefing
  • Communication plan
  • Emergency procedures
  • Required documents
  • Mission-specific preflight notes

What Should A Drone Preflight Checklist Cover?

Start with aircraft condition, local weather, airspace and restrictions, people and property near the operation, crew roles, control links, available power, and payload security. Add aircraft-specific checks and any conditions in an authorization, waiver, or agency procedure that apply to the mission.

14 CFR 107.49 describes preflight responsibilities for Part 107 operations. 14 CFR 107.15 addresses the aircraft’s condition for safe operation. Use the current rules and your aircraft manufacturer’s instructions alongside your own checklist.

UAV Preflight Checklist Questions

Is a UAV preflight checklist the same as a drone pre-flight checklist?

UAV, UAS, and drone are common terms used for these operations. A preflight checklist organizes the checks you perform before a flight; the details should match your aircraft, mission, and applicable procedures.

Does UAScribe include preflight checks in the report?

Yes. The completed preflight review and notes can be kept with the mission record and included in the PDF report you save, share, or print.

Can I use a printable checklist instead?

Yes. Download the printable PDF directly at the top of this page. Adapt it to your aircraft and operation; it is a starting worksheet, not an FAA-approved checklist.

Can the app approve my flight or obtain LAANC authorization?

No. UAScribe documents your review and provides informational lookups. The pilot remains responsible for required authorizations and the decision to fly.
